8.2
Environmental Monitoring of the Cleanroom
8.2.1
Active Air Sampling of the Cleanroom
8.2.1.1
Use TSA (Settle) plates to collect Active Air Sampling of the 
cleanroom.
8.2.1.2
Collect the active air sampling by following the steps below. 
8.2.1.2.1 Power on Active Air Sampler.
8.2.1.2.2 Label the TSA plates with analyst initials, date, 
location, and “active air”. For example: Active air 
sampling from cleanroom suite 115B inner ISO 7 
room will be labelled as “RS 01 Jul 2026 Active Air 
115B”. 
8.2.1.2.3 Unscrew the metal lid and uncover the slot for the 
TSA plate of the active air sampler.
8.2.1.2.4 Place the opened TSA plate in the slot.
8.2.1.2.5 Screw the metal lid of the active air sampler. The 
metal lid will go over the positioned TSA plate. 
8.2.1.2.6 Press the “CONFIGURATION” button on the home 
screen. 
8.2.1.2.7 Select Constant in the Mode menu item, then select 
total sampling volume to 1000L. If desired, also 
select an optional delay time (in seconds). 
8.2.1.2.8 Press the HOME button to return to the Home 
screen.
8.2.1.2.9 Press the START button to begin sampling and 
ensure that the total run time is 10 minutes.
8.2.1.2.10After sampling has been completed, unscrew the 
metal air sampler lid, retrieve plate and recover TSA 
plate with original lid.
8.2.1.2.11Twist the TSA plate lid and ensure that lid is locked 
and properly secured.
8.2.1.2.12Proceed to incubate plates as per 8.2.1.3.5
