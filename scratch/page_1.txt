
1.0
Purpose:
This procedure describes the requirements, methods, and documentation of nonviable 
particles and viable microbes monitored in the cleanroom facility at Eagle. 
2.0
Scope:
This procedure is applicable to the Anteroom (ISO 8), Cleanroom (ISO 7), and Biological 
Safety Cabinets (ISO 5) at Eagle.
 
3.0
Responsibilities:
3.1
It is the responsibility of microbiologists to follow this procedure.
3.2
It is the responsibility of the Quality Unit or designee to calculate the Contamination 
Recovery Rate (CRR), monthly through trending analysis.

3.2.2
If the incident rate is over the allowable percentage, an OOS shall be 
initiated with a complete process and operational investigation 
encompassing the following: review of cleanroom facility maintenance, 
sanitization/decontamination documentation, occurrence of non-routine 
events, the inherent physical or operational parameters (e.g., relative 
humidity, temperature changes and pressure changes) and the training status 
of all personnel.
3.3
It is the responsibility of Quality to ensure this procedure is followed as written.
4.0
Definitions:
4.1
Alert Level: A level of microorganisms that shows a potential drift from normal 
operating conditions and is based on historical data. 
4.2
Action Level: A higher level of contamination than the alert level that, if exceeded, 
is answered by an immediate follow-up, directed toward investigating the source of 
contamination and controlling it, resulting in a Corrective Action Response (CAR).
4.3
Anteroom: An ISO 8, or better area where personnel garbing, staging of components, 
and other designated activities are performed.
