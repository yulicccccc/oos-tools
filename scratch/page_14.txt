8.3
Action and Alert Investigations for Environmental Sampling
8.3.1
All microbial growth observed from environmental monitoring results shall 
be sent out for differential staining (if feasible) and identification to the 
species level.
8.3.1.1
Isolates found within the ISO 8 only need to be stained as per SOP 
2.600.020, Differential Staining, except in the following 
circumstances
8.3.1.1.1
Submit any isolates recovered from the ISO 8 area to the 
R&D Microbiology Lab for microbial identification, 
provided they are linked to samples that produced out-
of-specification results during the same testing session. 
8.3.1.1.2
Submit ISO 8 isolates if CFU is at or above the Action 
Level
Note: All fungal isolates recovered, regardless of ISO classification, must 
be identified to ensure appropriate documentation, risk assessment, and 
compliance with microbiological standards.
8.3.2
If the microbial contamination is greater than the designated action limit, 
follow SOP 2.100.019, Out-of-Specification (OOS) – Test Failure 
Investigation, to initiate an OOS investigation.
8.3.2.1
If a Sterility Test OOS occurs on the same day as the environmental 
monitoring OOS, a thorough investigation of the cleanroom 
facility, personnel, and other microbiological processes shall be 
initiated.
8.4
Action and Alert Investigations for Physical or Operational Parameters of the 
Anteroom and Buffer Room
8.4.1
Physical or operational parameters include relative humidity, temperature, 
and pressure of the cleanroom facility. These parameters are monitored 
electronically through the Lighthouse System. The action and alert levels are 
defined as such in SOP 2.700.014 Use of Lighthouse for Monitoring 
Environmental Conditions for the Houston location, and in SOP 2.700.059 
Use of Aircare Automation System for monitoring environmental conditions 
for the Canada Location.
