All analysts involved in the prepping, processing, and reading of the sample ‒ Andrew Carrillo, Guanchen Li and 
Elysse Nioupin, and were interviewed comprehensively. Their answers are recorded throughout this document.
 
Upon arrival, the sample was stored in accordance with the Client’s instructions. Analysts - Andrew Carrillo and 
Guanchen Li - verified the integrity of the sample throughout both the preparation and processing stages. No 
leaks or turbidity were observed at any point, verifying the integrity of the sample.
 
All reagents and supplies mentioned in the material section above were stored according to the suppliers’ 
recommendations, and their integrity was visually verified before utilization. Moreover, all reagents and supplies 
had valid expiration dates. The functionality of all equipment was confirmed by reviewing data generated by our 
comprehensive in-house continuous monitoring system.
 
The cleanroom suite (CR 115) used for processing procedures comprises three interconnected sections: the 
innermost ISO 7 cleanroom (115B), which connects to the middle ISO 7 buffer room (115A), and then to the 
outermost ISO 8 anteroom (115). A positive air pressure system is maintained throughout the suite to ensure 
controlled, unidirectional airflow from 115B through 115A and into 115.
 
The ISO 5 Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC) E001314, located in the innermost ISO 7 room, (115B), was used for 
sample processing steps. It was thoroughly cleaned and disinfected prior to each procedure in accordance with 
MICRO SOP-9 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology). Additionally, BSC E001314 was certified 
and approved by both the Engineering and Quality Assurance teams. Sample processing was conducted in the 
ISO 5 BSC E001314 in the innermost ISO 7 room, (115B) by Guanchen Li on 20 Aug 2026 as per MICRO-SOP-5 
(USP <71> / EP 2.6.1 Sterility Test).
 
On 20 Aug 2026, the sample vials for ETX-260811-0261 were received from the Sample Submissions team and 
brought into the Sterile Microbiology lab. Upon arrival, each sample vial was sprayed with an acidified bleach 
disinfectant, placed into pre-disinfected bins, and allowed a 10-minute contact time.

The secondary disinfection happened in the ISO 8 anteroom (115), where the vials were 
again treated with acidified bleach and provided a 10- minutes contact time before 
processing. Subsequently, the vials were moved into innermost ISO 7 cleanroom (115B). 
Inside this cleanroom, the processing analyst, Guanchen Li, performed a final disinfection 
step, allowing an additional 10-minute contact time. Once fully disinfected, the vials were 
transferred into the ISO 5 BSC E001314.
 
Inside the BSC, each container was aseptically opened, ensuring minimal exposure to the 
environment. The contents were then dispensed directly from the container into sterile 
canisters placed on a vacuum manifold for membrane filtration. The sample was processed 
and tested as per MICRO-SOP-5 (USP <71> / EP 2.6.1 Sterility Test). Media bottles were 
transferred into incubators E001356 and E001357 to begin incubation.
 
On 27 Aug 2026 (Day 7 of incubation), microbial growth was observed in one TSB (Tryptic 
Soy Broth) media bottle by analyst, Elysse Nioupin, and confirmed by Microbiology Lab 
Supervisor, Robin Seymour. The positive TSB bottle for ETX-260811-0261 was submitted for 
Differential Staining and Microbial Identification under ETX-260827-0547. Differential staining 
indicated the presence of Gram-positive rods. Definitive identification identified the isolate as 
Bacillus velezensis (Gram-positive rods).
 
 
The culture media utilized were within their expiry period. Negative control media bottles for 
the membrane filtration method were handled, processed, and incubated in the same manner 
as the test samples. No microbial growth was observed in the corresponding negative 
controls.
 
Upon analyzing the environmental monitoring results, no microbial growth was detected on 
personnel monitoring plates, settling plates, or ISO 5 BSC surface sampling plates on the 
date of testing, the preceding testing date, or the subsequent testing date. Additionally, no 
microbial growth was recovered from the weekly surface sampling plate collected on 20 Aug 
2026. However, weekly active air monitoring of ISO 8 Room 115 (CR 115) recovered 9 CFUs 
(ETX-260831-0366). Differential staining confirmed the organisms to be Gram-positive cocci.

It is important to note that the colony morphology of the microorganisms recovered from the cleanroom 
environment differed from that of the microorganism isolated from the test sample. This morphological 
distinction indicates that the environmental monitoring recovery and the test sample contamination were 
isolated and unrelated events.
 
In addition, considering the facility's layered disinfection program, controlled unidirectional airflow, and 
cleanroom design, it is unlikely that the microbial contaminant detected in the ISO 8 environment 
impacted on the sample processed within the ISO 5 area of the ISO 7 cleanroom (115B).
 
The absence of contamination on analyst glove plates and work-surface environmental monitoring 
plates collected on the date of testing indicates that there were no viable transfer of contaminants to the 
ISO 5 BSC. Additionally, the lack of contamination in other samples processed during the same testing 
session further demonstrates that the testing environment was operating in optimal condition.
 
Therefore, considering that no viable organisms were recovered from the ISO 5 environment, personnel 
monitoring results were acceptable on the date of testing, and the environmental isolates were not 
recovered from locations or surfaces directly involved in sample preparation or testing, the 
environmental isolates are not considered a credible source of the contamination detected in the test 
sample.
 
The analysts confirmed full compliance with cleaning procedures as outlined in MICRO SOP-9 
(Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology) and MICRO-SOP-5 (USP <71> / EP 2.6.1 
Sterility Test).
 
Monthly cleaning and disinfection of the outermost ISO 8 Anteroom, the middle ISO 7 Buffer room, the 
innermost ISO 7 cleanroom, and its containing ISO 5 BSCs for Cleanroom suite CR115 was performed 
on 30 Jun 2026, as per MICRO-SOP-9 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology). During 
both cleaning cycles, it was documented that all H₂O₂ indicators passed. This confirms the efficient 
monthly cleaning of all three parts of Cleanroom suite CR115.
 
Analyzing a 6-month sample history for Uriel Pharmacy (E02836), this specific analyte " Viscum Album 
20mg " has had no prior failures using USP <71> / EP 2.6.1 Sterility Test during this period.
 
To assess the potential for sample-to-sample contamination contributing to the positive results, a 
comprehensive review was conducted of all samples processed on the same day. All other samples 
processed by the analyst and other analysts that day tested negative. These findings suggest that 
cross-contamination between samples is highly unlikely.
 
Based on the observations outlined above, it is unlikely that the failing results were due to reagents, 
supplies, the cleanroom environment, the process, or analyst involvement. Consequently, the possibility 
of laboratory error contributing to this failure is minimal and the original result is deemed to be valid.
