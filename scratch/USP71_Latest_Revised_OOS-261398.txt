All analysts involved in the testing process were comprehensively interviewed, including the processing 
analyst, Qiyue Chen, and the reading analyst who confirmed the positive result, Andrew Carrillo. Their 
responses have been documented throughout this report.
 
The samples were stored as per the Client’s direction upon arrival. The processing analyst who performed the 
testing on 01Jun2026, 03Jun2026, 04Jun2026, and 08Jun2026, respectively, Qiyue Chen, confirmed that all 
sample containers were intact, and no visible particles were observed. These visual checks confirmed the 
sample's integrity.
 
All reagents and supplies used are mentioned in the material section above. All reagents and supplies were 
stored according to the suppliers’ recommendations, and their integrity was visually verified before utilization. 
Moreover, each reagent and supply had a valid expiration date. The functionality of all equipment was 
confirmed by reviewing data generated by our comprehensive in-house continuous monitoring system.
 
The samples were processed within the ISO 5 Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC), E001316, situated in the 
innermost clean room (Suite 114B), which connects to the intermediate ISO 7 buffer room (Suite 114A). This 
buffer room, in turn, leads to the outermost ISO 8 Anteroom (Suite 114). Furthermore, a positive air pressure 
system is maintained, ensuring airflow from the innermost ISO 7 cleanroom to the intermediate ISO 7 buffer 
room and finally to the outermost ISO 8 Anteroom. 
 
The innermost ISO 7 Clean-room Suite 114B, and the ISO 5 BSC E001316 within the Suite 114B were 
thoroughly cleaned and prepared before initiating the testing as per SOP 2.600.002 (Environmental 
Monitoring of the Clean Room Facility), and SOP 2.600.018 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for 
Microbiology). Furthermore, the BSC used for the testing, E001316, was certified and approved by the 
Engineering and Quality Assurance teams. 
 
Samples Disinfection Procedure
Upon receipt from the Sample Submissions team, the component sterility samples - DWK 50cc Sterile- Clear 
Glass Vials associated with ETX-260529-0274 and ETX-260601-0310 and DWK 3cc Clear Vials associated 
with ETX-260603-0062 and ETX-260605-0141 were transferred into the wet lab for preliminary disinfection. In 
accordance with SOP 2.600.008, the outermost plastic layers were sprayed with acidified bleach and allowed 
a 10-minute contact time. Following disinfection, the samples were placed into pre-disinfected bins and 
transferred to the ISO 8 anteroom (Suite 114).

From Suite 114 (ISO 8) to the innermost Suite 114B (ISO 7) and ultimately into the ISO 5 Biological Safety 
Cabinet (BSC), all outermost disinfected plastic layers were removed, and any additional plastic packaging 
layers were subjected to disinfection using acidified bleach, with a 10-minute contact time applied at each 
stage of handling. In contrast, Tyvek layers were not sprayed with acidified bleach during this process. 
Instead, Tyvek packaging was removed at designated cleanroom stages, in accordance with established 
aseptic handling procedures. This distinction in handling ensures that both packaging types are managed 
appropriately while maintaining sterility throughout the cleanroom transition.
 
Within the ISO 5 BSC, the last innermost layer was aseptically opened. Twenty vials for each sample were 
randomly selected using sterile forceps and transferred into media jars for sterility testing. 
 
The observations above and the confirmation from the processing analyst, Qiyue Chen, indicate that the 
samples under investigation, ETX-260529-0274, ETX-260601-0310, ETX-260603-0062, ETX-260603-
0062, and ETX-260605-0141 were cleaned, transferred to the Clean Room, processed, and tested as per 
2.600.008 (USP <71> / EP 2.6.1 Sterility Test) on 01Jun2026, 03Jun2026, 04Jun2026, and 08Jun2026, 
respectively. The media bottles were subsequently transferred into designated incubators, E001356 and 
E001357, to initiate incubation.
 
On Day 14 of incubation (15Jun2026,17Jun2026 and 22Jun2026), the samples associated with ETX-
260529-0274, ETX-260601-0310, and ETX-260605-0141 were found to be positive for microbial growth in 
one of the FTM (Fluid Thioglycollate Media) bottles, while on Day 12 of incubation (16Jun2026), the 
sample associated with ETX-260603-0062 was found to be positive for microbial growth in one of the TSB 
(Tryptic Soy Broth) bottles by Andrew Carrillo, and confirmed by Microbiology Lab Supervisor, Robin 
Seymour. 
 
The microbial IDs were confirmed to be Staphylococcus epidermidis, Cutibacterium acnes, and 
Dermacoccus nishinomiyaensis for ETX-260529-0274, ETX-260601-0310, and ETX-260603-0062 
respectively, while the microbial IDs associated with ETX-260605-0141 were Microbacterium paraoxydans
, Microbacterium sp. str. 'China', and Microbacterium sp. PAMC 28756. Details of the samples under 
investigation, including the reading analyst, day of incubation at which growth was observed, and microbial 
identification results, are summarized in Table 2 (see attached).
 
The culture media used were within their expiry period. During testing, the negative control media bottles 
were handled, processed, and incubated under the same conditions as the test samples. No microbial 
growth was observed in the corresponding negative controls, confirming the integrity of the test procedure.
Monthly cleaning and disinfection of the outermost ISO 8 Anteroom, the middle ISO 7 Buffer room, the 
innermost ISO 7 clean-room, and its containing ISO 5 Biosafety Cabinets were performed on 31May2026 
as per SOP 2.600.018 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology). It was documented that all 
H₂O₂ indicators passed. This confirms the efficient monthly cleaning of all three parts of Cleanroom 114.

Tables 3-6 (see attached) presents the environmental monitoring results. The environmental monitoring 
(EM) plates were incubated for no less than 48 hours at 30-35°C and no less than an additional five days 
at 20-25°C as per SOP 2.600.002 (Environmental Monitoring of the Clean-room Facility).
 
Upon reviewing the Environmental Monitoring results for the relevant testing dates, no microbial growth 
was observed on the ISO 5 BSC surface or settling plates, nor on the weekly active air and surface 
sampling plates from Cleanroom Suite 114 on the dates of testing, the dates prior and the subsequent 
testing dates. Additionally, both weekly active air sampling and weekly active surface sampling plates from 
both the week of testing and the week before testing, showed no microbial growth.
 
Review of the 6-month trend of past results for the specific sample analytes indicate that there is no other 
failing sample associated with “DWK 3cc Clear Vials SKU# 120 - Upright - [16 Facility], and DWK 50cc 
Sterile- Clear Glass Vials [SKU# 024] - [16 Facility].
 
To assess the potential for sample-to-sample contamination, all samples processed for USP <71> Sterility 
component testing in the same batch on 01Jun2026, 03Jun2026, 04Jun2026, and 08Jun2026 were 
reviewed (see Table 1). No other sample was found to be positive for microbial growth from the same 
batch of sample tested on respective days. Therefore, the probability of cross contamination among 
samples is negligible.
 
Although no microorganisms were identified from personnel or environmental monitoring samples, an 
adverse trend was observed. The lots that yielded failing results were tested by the same analyst within a 
span of a few days. Based on the historical analysis of samples from this client and the client’s sterilization 
process, it is highly unlikely that this number of lots would fail sterility testing in quick succession. Therefore
, the possibility of an unidentified analyst-related error contributing to the failing results cannot be ruled out. 
These findings were discussed with the client, and the client will submit additional samples from the same 
lots for retesting. These additional samples were received under submission IDs ETX-260618-0132, ETX-
260618-0133, ETX-260618-0134, and ETX-260624-0149. The results from the retest will be covered in the 
Phase 2 investigation summary.
