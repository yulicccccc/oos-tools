Environmental Monitoring Summary: Personnel monitoring plates for Guanchen (David) Li, including left- and right-touch plates, showed no microbial growth on the date before testing (03 June 2026), the date of testing (04 June 2026), and the date after testing (05 June 2026). For ISO 5 BSC E001314, daily surface sampling of four locations showed no microbial growth on the date before testing (03 June 2026). On the date of testing (04 June 2026), 1 CFU was recovered from Surface #1 associated with Guanchen (David) Li. The recovery was documented under ETX-260615-0424; microbial identification was not available at the time of review. Surface sampling performed on the date after testing (05 June 2026) showed no microbial growth. Settling sampling of ISO 5 BSC E001314, including two locations, showed no microbial growth on the date before testing (03 June 2026), the date of testing (04 June 2026), and the date after testing (05 June 2026). Weekly active-air monitoring of Suite 115 conducted during the week before testing (29 May 2026) showed no microbial growth. Weekly active-air monitoring conducted during the week after testing (05 June 2026) recovered 2 CFU in the ISO 8 area of Room 115 under ETX-260615-0432. The organism was identified as Staphylococcus epidermidis. Weekly surface monitoring of the anteroom and cleanroom areas associated with Suite 115 showed no microbial growth during the week before testing (29 May 2026) or the week after testing (05 June 2026). During the interview, the analyst indicated that no obvious abnormalities or deviations occurred during the testing process. All materials were disinfected before testing, and the relevant cleanroom and ISO 5 BSC were cleaned and prepared before testing in accordance with MICRO-SOP-2 and MICRO-SOP-9. Monthly cleaning and disinfection of the cleanroom suite, including the ISO 8 anteroom (115), ISO 7 buffer room (115A), ISO 7 cleanroom (115B), and the ISO 5 biosafety cabinets located within Room 115B, were performed on 26 April 2026 by Analyst - Rey Estrada - in accordance with MICRO SOP-9, Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology. All H₂O₂ indicators passed, confirming the successful completion and effectiveness of the monthly cleaning and disinfection activities within Rooms 115, 115A, and 115B. Additionally, routine cleaning and disinfection were performed before and after the testing activity in accordance with MICRO SOP-9. It is also important to note that no samples processed by Guanchen(David) Li in ISO 5 BSC E001314 on the date of testing (04 Jun 2026) failed sterility testing.  Based on the available evidence, one colony-like artifact was observed on Surface Plate #1 in ISO 5 BSC E001314 on the date of testing (04 June 2026). However, the observation could not be confirmed as a viable microbial CFU because no growth was obtained following inoculation onto fresh media. Therefore, the observation may represent a nonviable or non-microbial artifact, including a potential artifact associated with agar preparation or the agar-pouring process. If the observed artifact had represented a viable CFU, it would appear to be an isolated event. This assessment is supported by the absence of microbial recovery from Guanchen (David) Li’s personnel-monitoring plates on the date before testing (03 June 2026), the date of testing (04 June 2026), and the date after testing (05 June 2026); the absence of growth from BSC E001314 surface samples collected on the date before testing and the date after testing; and the absence of growth from ISO 5 settling plates throughout the bracketing period. Although active-air monitoring recovered Staphylococcus epidermidis in the surrounding ISO 8 area of Room 115 during the week after testing (05 June 2026), this recovery occurred outside the ISO 5 critical environment of BSC E001314. In addition, microbial identification of the ISO 5 Surface # 1 recovery was not available at the time of review. Therefore, a microbiological relationship between the ISO 5 surface recovery and the ISO 8 active-air recovery cannot be established.  The negative ISO 5 settling, personnel, and bracketing surface monitoring results demonstrate that the BSC E001314 critical environment remained in a state of control. The available data do not support migration, persistence, or recurrence of contamination within ISO 5 BSC E001314. Collectively, the evidence supports that the recovery was an isolated, transient, and non-recurring event, while established cleaning, disinfection, and aseptic controls remained effective.
