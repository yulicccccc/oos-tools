
8.1.3.2.2
After the testing process is finished, take the surface 
sampling from all four sites as shown in Figure 3 using 
RODAC/Contact plates. The sampling shall take place 
before the BSC is disinfected at the end of each testing 
process.
Figure 3. Surface Sampling Plan
8.1.3.2.3
Aseptically remove the contact plate’s lid and gently 
press the plate to one sampling site without twisting or 
sliding as shown in Figure 4. Ensure that the complete 
agar contacts the sampled surface.
Figure 4. Surface Sampling
8.1.3.2.4
Replace the contact plate’s lid.
8.1.3.2.5
Record the test performed on that date (e.g., Scan), 
sampling site (e.g., #1), BSC #, date, and initials of 
microbiologist on the base of the plate.
8.1.3.2.6
After sampling, thoroughly clean the sampled area 
with a 70% sterile IPA wipe.
8.1.3.2.7
Transfer the contact plate to the lab and log plates on 
the Surface Sampling Logbook (Form 3.600.002.F02). 
Plates must be logged within 24 hours of sampling.
8.1.3.2.8
Incubate the contact plate at 30-35ºC for NLT 48 hours 
and then at 20-25ºC for NLT 5 additional days.
