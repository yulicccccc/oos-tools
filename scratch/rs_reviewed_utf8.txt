All analysts involved in the prepping, processing, aliquoting, and reading of the samples – Guanchen Li (GL), 
America Alanis (ALA) and Cuong Du (CCD) were interviewed comprehensively. Their answers are recorded 
throughout this document. This investigation was performed as per MICRO-SOP-53 Sterility Test Out-of-
Specification (OOS) Investigation Procedure.
 
Upon arrival, the sample was stored in accordance with the Client’s instructions. Sample prepping and processing 
analyst -Guanchen Li (GL)- verified the sample's integrity throughout both the preparation and processing stages. 
No leaks or turbidity were observed at any point, verifying the integrity of the sample. 
 
All reagents and supplies mentioned in the material section above were stored according to the suppliers’ 
recommendations, and their integrity was visually verified before utilization. Moreover, all reagents and supplies 
had valid expiration dates. The functionality of all equipment was confirmed by reviewing data generated by our 
comprehensive in-house continuous monitoring system.
 
The cleanroom suite used for processing procedures (CR115) comprises three interconnected sections: the 
innermost ISO 7 cleanroom (115B), which connects to the middle ISO 7 buffer room (115A), and then to the 
outermost ISO 8 anteroom (115). A positive air pressure system is maintained throughout the suite to ensure 
controlled, unidirectional airflow from 115B through 115A and into 115.
 
Similarly, the cleanroom suite used for aliquoting procedures (CR114) comprises three interconnected sections: the 
innermost ISO 7 cleanroom (114B), which connects to the middle ISO 7 buffer room (114A), and then to the 
outermost ISO 8 anteroom (114). A positive air pressure system is maintained throughout the suite to ensure 
controlled, unidirectional airflow from 114B through 114A and into 114.
 
The innermost ISO 7 cleanroom 115B and its ISO 5 Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC) E001314 were thoroughly 
cleaned and prepared before initiating the testing by analyst Guanchen Li as per MICRO-SOP-2 (Environmental 
Monitoring of the Clean Room Facility), and MICRO-SOP-9 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for Microbiology). 
 
Similarly, for Celsis aliquoting, the ISO 7 cleanroom 114A and its ISO 5 BSC E001798 were thoroughly cleaned 
and prepared by analyst America Alanis before initiating the testing as per MICRO-SOP-44 and MICRO-SOP-9. 
 
Both the BSCs, E001314 in 115B and E001798 in 114A, were certified and approved by the Engineering and 
Quality Assurance teams prior to use.

On 13 Aug 2026, the sample vials for ETX-260812-0342 were received from the Sample 
Submissions team and brought into the Sterile Microbiology lab. Upon arrival, each sample vial was 
sprayed with an acidified bleach disinfectant, placed into pre-disinfected bins, and allowed a 10-
minute contact time. The secondary disinfection happened in the ISO 8 anteroom (115), where the 
vials were again treated with acidified bleach and provided a 10‑minute contact time before 
processing. Subsequently, the vials were moved into the ISO 7 cleanroom (115B). Inside this 
cleanroom, the processing analyst, Guanchen Li performed a final disinfection step, allowing an 
additional 10-minute contact time. Once fully disinfected, the vials were transferred into the ISO 5 
BSC E001314.
 
Once transferred into the ISO 5 BSC, the vials were placed on the disinfected working surface of 
the BSC E001314 and aseptically opened and tested in accordance with MICRO-SOP-44 (Celsis 
Sterility Testing). Following testing, the media bottles were subsequently transferred into designated 
incubators, E001356 and E001357, to initiate incubation. 
 
Upon completion of incubation on 20 Aug 2026, both TSB & FTM bottles were disinfected and 
transferred to the middle ISO 7 buffer room (114A) for aliquoting step per MICRO-SOP-44 (Celsis 
Sterility Testing). In cleanroom 114A, the media bottles were disinfected one more time before 
transferring them to the ISO 5 BSC E001798 located in cleanroom 114A. In ISO 5 BSC E001798, 
the sample was aliquoted into assay cuvettes by analyst America Alanis. After aliquoting, Celsis 
Sterility Reading was performed in accordance with MICRO-SOP-44 by analyst Cuong Du (CCD).
 
Following the reading, sample ETX-260812-0342 was found to yield a positive reading in one of the 
100mL FTM media bottles. The average Relative Luminescence Units (RLU) from the duplicate 
reading tube, originating from the FTM sample bottle, exceeded the average RLU of the FTM 
negative control, confirming a positive result. All other FTM and TSB sample bottles in same batch 
tested negative. The %CV from the duplicate reading tubes for the positive FTM bottles was well 
within the specification (< 30%). Additionally, all Daily Controls, including the Instrument Blank, 
Reagent Blank, and ATP Positive Control, were within the defined specifications, each with a %CV 
below 30%. 
 
Following the OOS result, the positive FTM bottle for ETX-260812-0342 was submitted for 
Differential Staining and Microbial Identification under ETX-260820-0454. Differential staining 
confirmed as Gram variable rods as cell morphology, while definitive identification confirmed 
Sporolactobacillus terrae. (see table 1).
 
The culture media utilized were within their expiry period. The negative culture media bottles for the 
membrane filtration method for the original culture were handled, processed, and incubated in a 
manner identical to that of actual samples. No microbial growth was observed in the corresponding 
negative control.

Monthly cleaning and disinfection of the outermost ISO 8 Anteroom, the middle ISO 7 Buffer room, the innermost ISO 7 cleanroom, and its containing ISO 
5 Biosafety Cabinets for CR115 and CR 114 was performed on 30 Jun 2026, as per MICRO-SOP-9 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for 
Microbiology). It was documented that all H₂O₂ indicators passed. This confirms the efficient monthly cleaning of all three parts of Cleanrooms 115 and 
114. 
 
Tables 2 & 3 (please see attached) present the environmental monitoring results for the duration of testing. The environmental monitoring (EM) plates 
were incubated for no less than 48 hours at 30-35°C and no less than an additional five days at 20-25°C as per MICRO-SOP-2 (Environmental Monitoring 
of the Cleanroom Facility). Table 2 pertains to Environmental Monitoring performed during Celsis Sterility Processing, and Table 3 pertains to 
Environmental Monitoring performed during Celsis Sterility Aliquoting.
 
Environmental Monitoring (EM) Results from Celsis Sterility Processing:
 
A review of the Environmental Monitoring (EM) results for the relevant Celsis Sterility processing period showed no microbial growth on personnel 
monitoring plates, settling plates, or ISO 5 BSC E001314 surface sampling plates on the date of testing (13Aug2026), the preceding sampling date 
(12Aug2026), or the subsequent sampling date (14Aug2026). Weekly active air and surface sampling of the cleanroom suite associated with processing 
was performed on 13Aug2026. No microbial recovery was observed from weekly surface sampling of the cleanroom. However, active air sampling 
showed recovery of 11 CFUs (ETX-260824-0353) in the ISO 8 area and 2 CFUs (ETX-260824-0360) in ISO 7 room 115A. The microorganisms recovered 
from active air sampling were identified as Nocardiopsis dassonvillei (Gram-positive filamentous rods), Micrococcus luteus (Gram-positive cocci), 
Staphylococcus epidermidis (Gram-positive cocci), and Talaromyces sp. (branching hyphae). The ISO 7 cleanroom (115A) recovery was identified as 
Micrococcus luteus (Gram-positive cocci).
 
Environmental Monitoring (EM) Results from Celsis Sterility Aliquoting:
 
After reviewing the Environmental Monitoring (EM) results for the relevant Celsis Sterility aliquoting period, no microbial growth was observed on 
personnel monitoring plates, ISO 5 BSC E001798 surface sampling plates, or settling plates on the date of aliquoting (20Aug2026), the preceding 
sampling date (19Aug2026), or the subsequent sampling date (21Aug2026). The Weekly environmental monitoring bracketing aliquoting date was not 
used in the evaluation of this OOS investigation given turbidity of media bottle from microbial growth was detected prior to aliquoting (see details under 
NCR N26436) .
 
Evaluation of Environmental Monitoring Results:
 
It is important to note that, for both processing and aliquoting activities, the samples are processed within validated ISO 5 BSCs (E001314 and E001798 
respectively). Although microbial recovery was observed from weekly active-air monitoring of the ISO 8 room 115 and ISO 7 background environments in 
the initial processing cleanroom suite (CR 115), these recoveries were not obtained from the ISO 5 processing environment. 
 
The absence of microbial recovery from both the processing and the aliquoting analyst glove plates, ISO 5 BSC surface monitoring plates, and ISO 5 
settling plates indicate that no identifiable contamination transfer pathway existed between the monitored background cleanroom environment and the 
ISO 5 critical processing areas. Samples were also prepared and transferred into disinfected, lidded containers and transported on carts within the 
controlled cleanroom environment. Therefore, while the active-air results demonstrate microbial recovery in the surrounding ISO 8 and ISO 7 areas, there 
is no evidence that the recovery originated from, or affected, the ISO 5 BSC environment where the test sample was handled and processed.
 
All analysts confirmed full compliance with cleaning procedures as outlined in SOPs MICRO-SOP-9 (Cleaning and Disinfecting Procedure for 
Microbiology), and MICRO-SOP-44 (Celsis Sterility Testing). A review of the available data confirms that the cleanroom and equipment conditions 
remained within acceptable parameters. No deviations or obvious signs of laboratory error during processing and aliquoting were noted from analysts.
 
 
No other sample processed by analyst GL on 13 Aug 2026 and aliquoted by analyst ALA on 20 Aug 2026, failed Celsis Sterility testing that day.
 
An analysis of the six-month sample history for Boudreaux's New Drug Store indicates that Eagle Analytical processed 406 samples for Celsis Sterility 
testing with one prior occurrence of a positive result during this period (Table 4).
 
A review of the lot history shows that there have been no additional submissions of sample lot: 227655 for retesting using Celsis Sterility Testing or any 
other sterility method.
 
To assess the potential for sample-to-sample contamination contributing to the positive results, a comprehensive review was conducted of all samples 
processed on the same day. No other sample processed by the same analyst on day of testing were found to be positive for microbial growth. These 
findings suggest that cross-contamination between samples is unlikely.
 
Based on the observations outlined above, there is no clear indication that the contamination originated from reagents, supplies, the cleanroom 
environment, or the analyst. Therefore, the results are considered valid, and the likelihood of laboratory error is minimal.
