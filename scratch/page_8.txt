8.1.3.3
Personnel Monitoring of ISO 5 BSC
8.1.3.3.1
Use TSA plates to collect Personnel Monitoring 
samples. 
8.1.3.3.2
The microbiologist who works in the ISO 5 BSC is 
required to touch each gloved fingertip of each hand on 
the surface of two different TSA plates using the 
rolling technique after each testing process (Figure 5).
Note: Do not re-touch the sampled areas and do not 
spray 70% IPA or bleach solution right before 
sampling. 
Figure 5. Personnel Sampling
8.1.3.3.3
Record the test performed of that testing process (e.g., 
Scan), touch plate (T), BSC #, left hand (LH) or right 
hand (RH), date, and initials of microbiologist on the 
base of the TSA plates.
8.1.3.3.4
Transfer TSA plates to the lab and log plates on the    
Personnel Sampling Logbook (Form 3.600.002.F03). 
Plates must be logged within 24 hours of sampling. 
8.1.3.3.5
Incubate the TSA plates at 30-35ºC for NLT 48 hours 
and then at 20-25ºC for NLT 5 additional days. 
8.1.3.3.6
Record the result of personnel monitoring on the 
Personnel Sampling Logbook (Form 3.600.002.F03).
Note: The action and alert levels for Personnel 
Monitoring are as follows: 
• Alert level: ≥ 1 CFU/Plate
